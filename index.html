Frequently asked questions

How does the scoring work?

Picture the shot you want to hit, hit it, and then judge only how well you executed it, not how it turned out. Score it from 1 (the worst quarter of your ability) to 4 (the best quarter). Tap your club, tap your score, and the app moves on to your next shot. Putts are scored too, and a short putt you simply tap in is scored as a Tap-in. A penalty shot is scored as Pen; long-press Pen to change it to Shank.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Your rounds, club names, and settings are never sent to a server, and I can't see them. See the Privacy Policy.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Recording and viewing rounds does not use the internet.

How do I back up my rounds?

Open the Setup tab and find Back Up Your Rounds. Tap Export JSON for a full backup, or Export CSV for a spreadsheet-friendly file. Then save it to Files or iCloud Drive, or send it to yourself by email or AirDrop. Restoring a backup into the app is not available yet.

How do I delete a round I saved by mistake?

Open the Setup tab, tap Round History, and tap Delete next to the round.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app from your phone. Because everything is stored on the device, this removes all of your data.

Can I rename my clubs?

Yes. Open the Setup tab and edit the club names in Your Clubs. Clear a name to hide a club you don't carry.

Can I change the encouragement messages?

Yes. They are under Hole Messages on the Setup tab. Clear a message to turn it off.
