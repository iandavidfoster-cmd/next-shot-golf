Privacy Policy

Next Shot Golf · Effective October 27, 2026

In short: Next Shot Golf does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any of your information. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Who I am

Next Shot Golf is published by Ian Foster ("I", "me"). You can reach me at ian.foster@live.com.

What the app stores on your phone

To work, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • the rounds you record (holes, shots, clubs, and scores);
  • your club names and the wording of your encouragement messages;
  • a setting that remembers whether you have dismissed the welcome screen.

This information never leaves your phone unless you choose to export it (see below). I do not receive it and cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes this information.

What the app does not do

  • It does not require an account or sign-in.
  • It does not collect personal information, your location, or your contacts.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • It does not include third-party advertising or analytics software.

Exporting your data

The app lets you export your rounds as a JSON or CSV file. You choose where the file goes, such as Files, iCloud Drive, email, or AirDrop. I never receive these files. Once you share a file, how it is handled is up to the service you send it to.

Links to other websites

The app contains a link to a third-party website (epiphanygolf.com). If you tap it, it opens in your web browser, and that website's own privacy policy applies. I am not responsible for other websites.

Apple

If you download the app through the App Store or TestFlight, Apple may collect information about the download under Apple's own privacy policy. I do not receive personal information from Apple about you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, for example if accounts or cloud features are added, I will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email ian.foster@live.com.
